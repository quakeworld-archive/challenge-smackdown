Sorry about the delay - Wart - 16/03/01

Most of the information that was on the page before the problems with the Challenge Network struck aren't viewable at the moment. It's not that they were lost (as the stats section is still there and up to date) but the backoffice stuff is in place so the old schedule, rankings and report pages are being replaced. I'll work on getting them up to speed in the next day or so, especially with finals just around the corner.

I'll quickly mention the matches that went ahead during the time that we couldn't update the page. The crucial game between List and THDM was played on Sunday night and, as expected, it turned out to be extremely close after going to a tiebreaker map. THDM took dm3 while List made an impressive comeback on dm2 to even things up...e1m2 was the map that would decide who went into the finals and a winner couldn't be picked until the very end. THDM managed to finish in front of List by less than 10 frags. Tuesday night saw Foad and Azul play their match and despite both teams already guaranteed to make the finals they didn't just go through the motions. Foad were too strong for Azul on dm3 but it was a lot closer on e3m7 with each team enjoying the lead for various amounts of time. Foad were ahead when it mattered though to wrap the game up in 2 maps.

This leaves Rb vs MU and Rb vs Lies as the remaining matches in Group 1 and iC vs FA the only one left in Group 2. Once we have confirmed dates and times we'll post them on here.
