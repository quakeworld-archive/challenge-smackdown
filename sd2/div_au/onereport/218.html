Playoffs
Fallen Angels VS Fuck off and Die


Round 1 - Wart

Fallen Angels 198
Fuck off and Die 99
E3M7 - The Haunted Halls (Fallen Angels's choice)


Demos

POV: harlsom

Report
This was a match that I had been looking forward to and it provided a close contest on this first map. FA managed to jump out to an early lead and got some guys to pent RL straight away. This tactic worked wonders against Azul and appeared to be paying off against Foad until 15 minutes remaining when Foad drew level again. For a few minutes Foad threatened to take over the map but FA were able to return to pent RL and kept Foad's lead to 10 or so frags before evening the scores again. It was almost frag for frag at this stage but FA slowly built up a lead which was reinforced when Harlsom got quad and pent. The rest of the map saw FA rack up the frags to finish ahead by a comfortable margin.



Round 2 - Wart

Fuck off and Die 47
Fallen Angels 273
DM2 - Claustrophobolis (Fuck off and Die's choice)


Demos

POV: harlsom

POV: spice

Report
Once again FA had a good start to the map as they grabbed the first few quads. This time there was no comeback by Foad even though they managed to steal the occasional quad...FA just secured RLs for their players which made it easier to launch attacks. The score at the 10 minute mark was 119 - 25, and even when Blind dropped near the end with 57 frags FA still had a fair lead. There was no change in the trend of the map with FA wrapping up another finals victory.
