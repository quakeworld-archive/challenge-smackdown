CG vs CP3 - zzjohnzz - 12/02/01

The most annoying thing about tonight is the fact I was outbid by some lamer at the last minute on EBAY. I collect rare games and placed a bit of $135 on a piece of software. The max bid was $5.50 when I left to admin and when I get back some sniper out bids me by $2.00.

Unlike my Ebay experience, the game tonight went rather smoothly. Both teams chose dm3, and they also agreed on the Anti-clan server where CG had an average ping of 74 and cp3 had pings around 210. CG is one of the best "dm3 clans" in NA. CP3 likes dm3 but are mainly hpb. Most readers can guess by now what the results were, a 2-0 stomping by CG.

CP3 started off game 1 with a 4-1 lead. That would be the only lead they would see all night. At the 2 min mark, CG was up 8-3 and at the 5 minute mark it was gg with CG leading 65-7. Amp was at the ra, gator was timing quad, getafix was controlling the ya and ecoli was in the box and there was nothing cp3 could do but spawn and die. Getafix finished with 74 frags, Gator had 73, and Amp had AN AMAZING 97% efficiency. Game 1 to CG 271-58.

Game 2 was more of the same minus the good start for CP3. CG let a few pents slip away, but that didn't seem to matter at CG wins 223-29. Gator finished with 68 frags and Ecoli had 63.

The next games to be played are: rb vs ag, cg vs xx, dg vs fff.
