AG vs ST - zzjohnzz - 17/03/01

Out-manned and out-pinged sums up the AG vs ST game. ST only had 3 show up for this one while AG had 6 people. The games would be played 4v3 with dm2 being the choice of AG and e1m2 being chosen by ST.

On dm2 AG got the 1st quad and rl and then def killed everything in his way to get control of the lower rl. Once the second lower rl was locked it was a spawn fragging nightmare for ST. They did play an intelligent game at lower rl, by flooding it and continually weakening the lower rl player for AG and managed to get a few rl's that way. Unfortunately def would come in with quad and clear things out. Game 1 to AG 370 - (-5)

At the start of the e1m2 game a strange force was felt on the server. Babies started to cry, I could hear wolves howling at the moon, leaves rustled in the wind and a certain sense of darkness and dispair overcame everyone on the server. Yes the entity known as Gibz had decided to make its presence felt at this event. Fear immediately struck all those around. ST then said that they wanted to change maps to dm3 but this was not allowed. I have never seen Gibz die on e1m2 and this game would be no exception. He camped the MH and anyone that tried to enter was cast under a stone curse spell. The poor player dumb enough to enter the lair of the Gibz was frozen in terror. Gibz would confuse his opponents and killed them over and over in route to 100% efficiency. Def also finished with 100% efficiency and 61 frags. ST played an ultra cautious game here trying to ambush anyone that entered the start area. But unfortunately they were out-pinged 65 to 147 and were down a man. AG with Gibz was up a demi-god and won the game 181-16 and the match 2-0.
