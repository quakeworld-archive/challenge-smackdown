Challenge Smackdown People


The People Behind Challenge Smackdown - Jjonez - 25/02/01

The current core team of Challenge Smackdown consits of (in alphabetical order):

  • Apollyon - Germany
    Involved in all things QW in Germany, for example Ownage and ESPL.
    Leader of the North European division.

  • Gacel - Spain
    Devoted QuakeWorld player and leader of the Spanish QW Clan Guerreros de la Virtud. Webmaster and president of the Spanish QuakeWorld League (LEQW) since January 2000.
    He was admin for the South European division in season 1 but now in charge of programming the backoffice. 'QuakeWorld is pure art'

  • Jjonez - Belgium
    Oldtime QW enthusiast and writer at Challenge-World. More info here.

  • Judge Dredd - Brazil
    Co-founder of Challenge-BR. Plenty more Dredd trivia here.
    Leader of the Brazilian division.

  • Tekai - Germany
    The hmtl & gfx guru; QW is still his greatest passion.

  • Wart - Australia
    Runs his own lans in Melbourne... QW only of course.
    Leader of the Australian division.

  • Znappe - Sweden
    Znapp from Stockhom; 18 years old and player (even of Q3 and CS) in clan Blisters. Leader of the Nordic All-Stars that will be restarted later this year. Worked as a game admin in Smackdown 1.

  • ZtrANgeR - Portugal
    20 year old QW addict, playing for Rebellion of Thunder since he created it. Administrated/presided some Portuguese QW Leagues.
    Leader of the South European division.

  • ZzJohNzZ - United States
    Long time QW enthusiast, custom map beta tester and reviewer, webmaster of three's a crowd.

Special thanks to CykloP for creating the website design!

People whos help during Season 1 was invaluable:

  • Andy - Germany
    Experienced league admin from UKQA, UKCL and Villains. Still loves QW and wants it to be the most played game again!

  • Egowhip - Sweden
    Founder of the most succesful Villains on-line QW league.

  • JP - Portugal
    A Portuguese QW'er that loves the game sooo much ...
