Challenge Smackdown PAK3 Contest


PAK Contest - Gacel - 15/03/01

Tired of your predefined console background?, tired of those fullbright skins red and white?, tired even of the crosshair? x)))

The Challenge-SmackDown crew is proud to present the SmackDown PAK Contest.

If you feel skilled enough, try to create a C0oL PAK and send it to me. We are looking for:

  • Nice console backgroud
  • Font (+leds)
  • Scoreboard text (ranking)
  • Skins that look GOOD
  • Menu text
  • HUD graphics
Remember! this is the most important: the PAK must NOT mess with QIZMO's f_modified command.

Of course the PAK be done in a "SmackDown style", for example some kind of logo in the skins would be good.

I'll post here screenshots of the PAKs that you send me. Also all PAKs will be ready to download.

The PAK winner of the contest will be set as the "Official SmackDown PAK", and its creator will achieve fame and glory ;).

Good luck!
