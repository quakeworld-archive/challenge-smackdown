Challenge Smackdown North Europe

1/4 Finals
Overdoze VS The Viper Squad


Round 1 - Apollyon

Overdoze 109
The Viper Squad 163
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Overdoze's choice)


Demos

POV: blaze

Report
TVS got a start i haven't seen in a long time, they all spawned at the RA side of the map near the tele. Despite their "full start" OD couldn't use the powerups to lock down any areas early on. After one minute the score was just 10 to 9 in favour of OD. Until the second penta the scores were almost equal when OD took the pentagram and the quad as well. Seconds later they were leading by 11 frags. OD secured both YA and RA and took the next quad as well further increasing their lead. TVS took the last quad before penta and used it to secure RA. OD got the pent though but it didn't do much damage while TVS secured YA as well and took the next quad, too. TVS then took the lead with 76 to 75 frags. That didn't last long since OD took another quad and used it to get the RA back under their control. But soon TVS took it over again and got the last penta as well. From then on TVS kept RA and quad under firm control and OD only managed to annoy them at YA a few times. TVS finally won with a rather comfortable 54 frags lead.



Round 2 - Apollyon

Overdoze 76
The Viper Squad 249
DM2 - Claustrophobolis (Overdoze's choice)


Demos

POV: blaze

Report
It took TVS several minutes until they got the upper hand over OD here on Claustrophobopolis. TVS started with an ealry lead but quickly OD secured quad and YA/tele while TVS were pushed back to the lower rl. After 4 minutes TVS were still leading 28-19 but OD were still controlling the quad side of the map. About a minute later TVS got a quad and used it to secure high rl. Now the lead of TVS is increasing while they respawnfrag their opponents for some minutes. But OD come back, take a quad and after 10 minutes TVS leads by 62 frags but it does not look like it is going to be an easy victory. During most of the remaining 10 minutes TVS have the upper hand in the all important quad and YA/tele areas. Overdoze got a few quads and lots of rl's at low rl but could't lock down anything for a longer time. In the end TVS wins with a comfortable margin but they had to fight hard for most of their frags.
TVS advance to the semifinals where they will meet FF.
