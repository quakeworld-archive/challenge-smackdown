Group 1
Fuck off and Die VS Lies


Round 1 - Porto

Fuck off and Die 268
Lies 23
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Fuck off and Die's choice)


Demos

POV: darkone

POV: hashie

Report
foad started out with their map : dm3 and after a even first couple of minutes most of foad had emerged with rls and went about putting their strategy into effect. they had hashie and reign spending most of their time at the water/ya sections, while darkone and charlie spent time on the ra and killing a number of spawns in the centre area while running quads. this proved to be quite effective and lies were unable to inflict much damage in the following few minutes. towards the half-way poing some of lies had resorted to using the axe but soon concentrated again and managed to regain control of the water side allowing them to stock up some supplies and even some quads. the situation was repaired fairly quickly by foad before lies could cause a large amount of trouble and remove all enemy rls.



Round 2 - Porto

Lies 120
Fuck off and Die 195
E1M2 - Castle of the Damned (Lies's choice)


Demos

POV: darkone

POV: hashie

Report
lies' map, e1m2, was a much closer affair from the outset. lies seemed to move better in groups on this map and due to this foad werent able to dominate as they did on dm3. resources were fairly evenly shared, including the quad, rl and ya, and heading into the second half of the map foad only had a lead of about 30. unfortunately lies could not close the gap however, and foad slowly extented their lead as hashie made his way up the scoreboard and reign got his hands on a number of quads. a much closer match but foad still too strong in the end, closing it out with a comfortable margin.
