Challenge Smackdown North Europe

Group C
Noskill VS Clan MalFunction


Round 1 - Crit

Noskill 90
Clan MalFunction 191
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Noskill's choice)


Demos

POV: vert

Report
First map was dm3, choosen by n0skill. Cmf got pent and quad, ns 1st rl. Having quad and pent turned out to be better this game, cause soon cmf took control over the ra area and first 2 quads. None of the team really locked the map in the minutes after that, but most of the time ra was controlled bij cmf, ns managed to nick some quads tho:) after the 3th pent cmf really got a tight grip on the map, and they increased their lead. The last pent taken bij ns, and with that they took control of the map and madeup some frags. But it was already 150-70 by then, so to late to win the this map.



Round 2 - Crit

Noskill 107
Clan MalFunction 222
DM2 - Claustrophobolis (Noskill's choice)


Demos

POV: jester

Report
2nd map was dm2, cmf's choice. Cmf got a fullstart and quickly took control over the tele/water/quad area, and ns pulled back to low rl. This situation remains for the next 3 minutes. After that ns pressure on quad gets heavier resuling in ns taking their first quad in the 15th minute. The quaddude gets killed soon tho, but ns had control over the tele area after that, and takes the next quads, and even securing low rl. But that didnt lasted very long, soon cmf takes over the tele again. But because of some back luck (2 cmf players telefragged eachother with rl) ns manages to take the next two quads, and make alot of kills with it. After that, none of the team really had control, but cmf had the upperhand and fragged the most. With 3 minutes to go, ns is able to seize control again, but it was to late, cause the fragdifference was to big already. Cmf wins quite comfortable: 222 - 107, but they sure werent much better then ns. gg!
