Demos...and more - Wart - 16/02/01

Sorry about the delay...but I've finally been able to upload the demos I've had so far to Challenge.TV but there's one or two games that I still have to chase up. You can either go to Challenge.TV normally and then go to the "QuakeWorld" section, go straight to the Smackdown 2 section or you can use the "latest Smackdown demos" feature up the top there (though I think I put too many demos in for that to be useful right now). One thing we've noticed is that a few teams haven't been recording demos even though the winning team is supposed to submit at least one. In each match that I've been present at (even if I'm not running it) the admin has reminded the players to record right before the countdown but somehow people still manage to forget. If people only remember as the timer is counting down and think that they don't have enough time to start recording then they can simply type .er to use the "Easy Record" feature. This takes care of the naming for you and even stops the demo when the map changes at the end. Another reason why people might not want to record is that they don't want others getting their demos for whatever reason...if this is a problem then you can ask us the admin not to post the demo on Challenge.TV and they'll make sure it doesn't go anywhere. We partially need the demos for Porto's statistics but teams are making it hard for him to keep them accurate.

One of the teams expressing interest to replace FF is Stupify who are made up of NZ players with some Aussies thrown in as well. Here's the line-up -
Stupify (SPY) - Dakaha, Reptile, Dalcon, Deebo, Ch[ill]98, Mazon, Deathrow, Akuma, Wizzy, Corrupt, Soop

We're looking into one other team to take Justice's spot but since today is the deadline that I put in place for replacement teams we'll just look at these two and if either don't work out then we'll just stay as we are.
