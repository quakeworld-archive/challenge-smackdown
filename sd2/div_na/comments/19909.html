gq vs st - zzjohnzz - 27/04/01

When the main megahealth master of mayhem isn't around, who you gonna call? The ghost busters are out of business so the cobra had to step up to the plate and defend the 100 health. Anti said he could play "in a bit" and "in a bit" usually means 5-10 hours from now. So anti didn't make it to the game :( GQ faced off vs ST in an exciting 3 game battle. Game 1 was on e1m2 and GQ won 144-52. On dm6, the cobra, aka Mr Jenna Jameson, worked over the MH room with the precision that Jenna worked over Randy West in her last video. Cobra finished with 107 frags as GQ won dm6, 316-151. E3M7 was a very exciting game. ST held the pent area for 6 minutes and the game scores were very close. GQ then got control of both sides of the board and started to dominate the game. Titan doesn't like to be dominated so he got the next pent and with his mad hpb skillz he killed 3 GQ's. He was running after the 4th, but the lpb GQ player got away. Titan then stopped and typed "ru npussy" which made everyone laugh. GQ got the last laugh though as they won e3m7 by a score of 125-65 and the match 3-0.
