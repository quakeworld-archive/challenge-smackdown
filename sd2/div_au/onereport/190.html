Round 1 - Sparky

Lies 128
Mal Azul 101
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Lies's choice)


Demos

POV: pricide

POV: bm

Report
After about half an hour of organising players and checking pings DM3 was chosing to be the first map. The first five minutes of the game was fairly close score wise, but that changed with Lies pushing ahead to get a rather large lead. Azul tried their best to make a comeback and recovered a lot of their lost frags but Lies managed to take dm3 by 27 frags.



Round 2 - Sparky

Mal Azul 153
Lies 141
E1M2 - Castle of the Damned (Mal Azul's choice)


Demos

POV: bm

Report
E1M2 was chosen as the 2nd map. Azul got a nice lead towards the first half of the match but Lies managed to come back and equal the scores. The last five minutes was frag for frag with Azul just pulling through at the end to win by 12 frags.



Round 3 - Sparky

Lies 88
DM2 - Claustrophobolis (random map)


Demos

POV: bm

Report
