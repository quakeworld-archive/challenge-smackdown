Round 1 - Porto

DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Fuck off and Die's choice)


Demos

POV: hashie

POV: orion

Report
these games were played 3on3 due to mu having one member absent on the evening. the ping problems to optus for bpa users and almost any other server for optus users caused us some problems in organising a server for the match. most of mu were comfortable on the ipgn server, but foad were pinging much better to optus. in the end we came to the agreement that each team would pick their map and play it on their server of choice, so we began with foad playing dm3 at optus.

foad started out quite well, managing to secure themselves some armors and weapons very early, and then denying mu armors as the match took form. darkone was controlling the ra side then making his way out to take a quad and head to the areas he believed had enemies, while at the same time hashie was making sure ya was under control. this left orion to do some exploring of the map. mu did for a short while manage to cause some trouble at the water, aided by an improvement in pings, but foads control of the map remained in place for the duration.
