Tribe of Tjernobyl VS Oh My God


Round 1 - mirco

Tribe of Tjernobyl 134
Oh My God 89
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Tribe of Tjernobyl's choice)


Demos

POV: slabby

POV: janus

Report
dm3:
OMG gets a good start with ring/pent/quad while TOT grabs the rl. OMG gets the second quad but TOT gets control over ra with two rl dudes. TOT grabs the second pent. OMG succeed in getting quad/rl but cant really handle the strong ra camping by TOT. OMG makes a lot of strong quad runs and are very close to get control of ra several times. But two TOT with rl at ra is too much. OMG gets the third pent after a nice takover and TOT is forced back to ya. When its 8 mins to go TOT is in the lead by 46 frags. The last pent goes to TOT. 2 mins left and OMG controls the ra. The last 2 mins is totally dominated by OMG, but there isnt any time for a catch up. Final score is 134 - 89 for TOT
