First Games Scheduled - JudgeDredd - 15/02/01

We have one more time scheduled games, this time for this weekend will be Rage Clan versus Hell Raiser and RS Terminators versus Papa-Figo. No private servers will be hosting the games, we are going back to public servers. It has been a long long time the last game I attended in a public server.

As long as I can say, Terminators and the Rage will be entering monday and the start of the week with a win. Perhaps you can remember I said these two teams hold the two best players in the country, Simps for Rage and Carlos for Terminators, both champions of the 1st NQA along with the Lords of War.

We are not going to have surprises unless they came too much confidants to the field and then yes Hell Raisers and Papa-Figo will have a good shot. Talking about Papa-Figo it's good to see these guys back in action, and why not steal a good one from the Terminators?
