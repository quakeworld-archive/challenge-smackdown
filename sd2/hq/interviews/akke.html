Akke: ff member - dK

Does ff have a basic strat on the various maps, such as akke at rl, dag at ra pietro quad etc.. or do you guys change your strat depending on the team you are up against?

We kind of ask each other minutes before the game where ppl wanna be etc :)... but depending on the opposition we give priority to different areas. We know some clans are very keen on having RA on dm3, so we adjust ourselves in order to defend RA successfully.. and so on...

What is your favorite sd2 map?

I have no real favourite, I love dm3 for its open areas and the speed, I love dm2 because it is so teamplay-demanding and I love e1m2 for the total mayhem it brings. :)

Is there a sd2 match that you consider ff's best performance?

I think we did pretty good vs tVS in the semi and vs ToT in the quarter. Both games were tight and in the end we managed to win them through great teamplay and individual sacrifices.

Who was ff's toughest opponent in sd2?

Must have been Firing Squad :)... they managed to win e1m2 vs us, don't ask me how that happened - I wasn't playing :)

Last question: Will ff participate in SD3?

Ofcourse!
