Group D
Tribe of Tjernobyl VS America Must Die


Round 1 - Apollyon

Tribe of Tjernobyl 116
America Must Die 114
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Tribe of Tjernobyl's choice)


Demos

POV: psycho dad

Report
The game was played on a "Ghettonet" server where both teams hat almost equal conditions. I was noticed of the game very short in advance and did not know what to expect except a good game. In the end it turned out not to be only a good game but actually one of the best and closest games i have ever watched. The teams began playing on dm3, TOT's choice. AMD have the better start by getting the pentagram and securing yellow armor area with that while TOT's quad is quickly dispatched. After the first minute AMD lead by 6 frags. But TOT get the second quad and attack ya with it while they had secured ra as well. Now it is AMD's turn to take the third quad but the bearer gets boomsticked. However scores are equal at 15 frags for each team. The situation on the map now looks like this. TOT are at the ra side of the map while AMd have the other side with quad and ya. From this classic situation AMD launch attacks on ra and Vovik is finally able to take and hold it. Even though AMD are now leading by frags TOT are constantly fragging elsewhere and AMD can't lock down the map. After 5 minutes AMD lead with 37-20 but TOT get the pentagram with a bit of luck. Meanwhile they had taken quad as well but the quaddamge got fragged by AMD at ra. The pentagram helps them to retake the lead however but AMD play very clever and TOT can't pull ahead of them really. Five minutes later TOT take the next pentagram as well after a fight over it which was described as "it's raining men" by a spectator. From now on TOT had quite good control over the map and were fragging faster than previously. After 12 minutes its 77-52 for TOT. Later AMD attack ra and take it again then go for the quad and take it as well. Now they are closing in on TOT again. TOT concentrate on getting the last penta as well and get it at last. But AMD emerges victorious out of the visious fight over ra. 16 minutes into the match and TOT only lead by two 92-90. AMD take quads now and frag everything that moves at ra with it. Shortly after they take the lead for the first time. With two minutes to go they lead by two frags only. TOT get the second but last quad but they can't retake ra with it where AMD are waiting for them. One minute later AMD still lead by one frag and the last quad is fragged without being usefull and tieing the score at 112-112. AMD are launching a last desperate attack after waiting fot the last red armors and make the last two frags in the game but TOT wins with 116-114.



Round 2 - Apollyon

Tribe of Tjernobyl 179
America Must Die 174
E1M2 - Castle of the Damned (Tribe of Tjernobyl's choice)


Demos

POV: oddjob

Report
After the breathtaking first map AMD's choice was to be played and it proved to be a thriller as well - even twice. The first time the teams played another tense match but after 16 minutes AMD's connection died (they played from an internet cafe). TOT were leading by just 1 frag at that time and when AMD returned 5 or 6 minutes later we decided that it would be fairest to start the map over. Although TOT get the first quad AMD starts better and after the first minute they were leading by 5 frags. But soon TOT managed to secure ya and slowly closed in on AMD. They are taking the quads however and with them push TOT back to ya. After five minutes AMD lead 33 to 30. While TOT were controlling ya and AMD quad all the fighting was going on at gl but neitjer team managed to break through. After 6 minutes Oddjob from TOT manages to snatch a quad but only with the sng his attack on mega is not very successful and the overall struggle for control went on for now. After 9 minutes AMD were leading by 13 frags and slowly increased that lead in the following time until up to 20 frags. With 8 minutes to go Oddjob is able to start the first real quadrun for TOT and take the lead for his team. Now TOT are controlling the map quite good and increase their lead steadily. Somewhat later AMD start an excellently timed attack on ra where the four of them easily defeat the sole defender of that area. Having four people there now they are still 21 frags behind TOT. But AMD take the next quad and quickly gain on TOT now. They are tying the score at 153-153. The game gets real tense now as TOT takes one of the last quads and increase their lead to 5 frags while there is just 1 minute left to play. AMD get the last quad but TOT have the yellow armors . 15 seconds to go and the game is still tied at 173-173. Despite AMD-Glad having the quaddamage TOT frag better during the final showdown at the gl area and win by merely 4 frags. Well it was an excellent game by two excellent clans. Both teams showed that they are really worth of getting into the playoffs and will surely make it in the end. And like mentioned before this was one of the best games i have ever watched. GG!
