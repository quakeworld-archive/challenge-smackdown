Group C
Noskill VS Armageddon Deep Town 2


Round 1 - tekai

Noskill 222
Armageddon Deep Town 2 43
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Noskill's choice)

No Screenshots
Demos

Report
Noskill got the first rl and quad, ADT2 got the pent. The ra-area was controlled by NS but they didn't succeed in taking the first quads. The fourth quad was securely guarded by numerous rl's, still it was a tough fight before NS could pick it up. 16 minutes to go NS in the lead with 45-19, at the 15 minute mark they pick up both penta and quad so everything looks to be over for ADT2. 7 min into the match NS is leading with 50 frags. After a big fight at penta ADT2 manages to take it, but it ends up at a player without any significant weapons and therefore he doesn't manage to do great harm to NS. The server lagged some but when a russian clan is on a server that always seem to happen :) With 6 minutes to go NS was in the lead with 100 frags, they got the penta and the following quad and just ran around looking for more frags. This game was decided by the time of the first penta, the rest of the match was just a matter of time until NS had won.



Round 2 - tekai

Noskill 196
Armageddon Deep Town 2 140
E1M2 - Castle of the Damned (Noskill's choice)

No Screenshots
Demos

Report
Ns took early command of the game. After 2 minutes they were in the lead with 40-7. The server showed its true nature with constant pl from 10-25 for everyone. ADT2 racked up some rl's and camped mega. When it was time for quad they stormed it and got it. The quadrunner was killed quickly though. The score was 60-29 to NS. NS got the next quad but it was killed quickly too. 11 minutes to go ADT2 was regaining some ground, only 20 frags up now. They managed to narrow the gap even further down to 10 frags. NS were keeping control of ya most of the time and it started to pay off. A NS quad cleared out some russians and NS started to gain frags again. With 5 minutes left they were 30 frags ahead. During the remainder of the match NS pull ahead some more.
