Animaniacs VS Full Connect


Round 1 - JudgeDredd

Full Connect 222
Animaniacs 318
DM6 - The Dark Zone (Full Connect's choice)


Demos

Report

If you think Castle of the Damned is a fast deathmatch map just wonder about The Dark Zone hosting a 4 on 4 skirmish. Non stoping action, as it could not be different, from the start of the game til it's end. You can't dominate zones in the DM6 when weapons stay is on, so in this case particularly best individual skilled players will always win and the maniacs have good talented soldiers.



Round 2 - JudgeDredd

Animaniacs 140
Full Connect 126
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Animaniacs's choice)


Demos

Report

Do or die situation for the Full Connect team and it's not good to face this having guys like this for foes, but there are no complains here, you must fight. Here one more time the Full Connect began another drama as the one against the lords but this time they at least in the beginning of the match took a good lead, 43 to 20 over 5 minutes of match. Naturally the Animaniacs never, never stop trying and this time they don't pretend also as they started to reach the FCs in the scoreboard trailing by 10, 54 to 64, close to the ten minute mark. About 13 minutes of game the match is tied at 84 and believe it or not the game was decided by the last Quad possession where the maniacs got it and eliminates few chances left for the Full Connect team to react in the final moments. Very good game.
