Almost done... - Apollyon - 04/04/01

There are not so many games left and i think its a good time to take a look at each group to see who is most likely to go into playoffs:

Group A:
There is no way around Flaming Fist winning this group. They have finished all matches but one and even if they loose that one (which is unlikely imo) they will be first in that group. What is way more interesting in that group is who will get the other three spots behind FF. Currently Firing Squad from holland look like they can get second. They played very strong so far beating some very good teams in form of Orkney Clan (with a bit of luck though) or Daltons and even snatching a map from FF. They still have two matches to go against Blisters an The Dark Icemarines both of which they are going to win in my opinion. The Dark Icemarines on the other hand didn't play very much yet and they could still get second if they win all reminign matches. Both Daltons and Orkney Clan are also playoff candidates and the match between those might decide upon who of the two advances to the playoffs.
Except the number one position this group will be exciting to the very last game while FS, OC, D and TDI are fighting over three spots.

Group B:
Again the number one postions seems to be reserved for one of the qw daddies, this time for The Viper Squad. Only one match remains for them against inneR Circle who were doing surprisingly well themselves despite their ping problems. Close on their heels is Clan Koff who are getting stronger and stronger. But RC beat them earlier in the season giving them a slightly better position to get second in this group. Lithium are the fourth clan here who are most likely to make it. All other just lost too many matches already. Despite that it proved to be a group with some of the most interesting matches until now.

Group C:
This group is still very open, the main reason for that being the one with the fewest played matches. At the moment Noskill are leading the board simply because they played the most matches. But they lost to both Overdoze and Clan MalFunction and i think its going to be these two who will determine the first place here. Both have a perfect record so far but still more than 50% of their matches left especially the one against each other. The fourth team here might be the BadBoys or Four Kings. Again it is most likely that it will be decided in the game between those two who will advance.

Group D:
Here two clans are more or less already qualified for the playoffs. Euthanasia continue to dominate everyone much like FF does in group A. Tribe of Tjernobly are the runner ups loosing only to E so far and having just one game left versus the strong Hellfire clan. Those are the only ones who can still compete with TOT for the second place. The remaining spot(s) will either go to Oh My Good or Paranoia. The latter team seems to have a slight advantage since their remaining matches look easier and they already won over OMG too. But maybe the russain team Amarica Must Die will be the laughing third. They played some very close matches (AMD-TOT come to mind) and showed that they can compete with the best clans in this league.

Overall we have not so many matches left, but we can be sure there are many exciting matches coming up some of which will surely have "playoff" quality.
