Some stats - Apollyon - 09/02/01

Two days ago we started to track the site statistics courtesy of "eXTReMe Tracking". You can find a link to comprehensive statistics on the lower left.
The numbers already show that challenge-smackdown is one of the largest sites within the challenge network. For examples we get more visitor than challenge-au and i am sure we will make it on top until the end of the season. Among other interesting things like where exactly people came from, which hard and software they use there is one striking category, the referrer tracking. It shows what kind of keywords in a search engine were used by people who ended on one of the smackdown pages. There are common ones like "clan tdi" and "qw edome servers" on neu but on au we have "spanked+-sex+-eroctic" and finally "hacked+qizmo". Very interesting indeed.
