EG vs CG - zzjohnzz - 23/03/01

This would decide the final playoff spot and the dm3 and e1m2 games were quite exciting. It turns out that CG would win dm3 and EG would win dm6 and e1m2. CG was making an incredible comeback on e1m2 but with 4 minutes to go their quad guy blew himself up allowing EG to get a rl/ya and then take the next quad and the match. Here are the scores:

dm3 155-122 for cg
dm6 360-222 for eg
e1m2 166-115 for eg

And believe it or not at this incredibly late stage of smackdown 2 not everyone has their qizmo troubles sorted out.

Here are the top 6 teams to make it into the A division: 1) AG 2) XX 3) GQ 4) EG 5) DG 6) ST the rest of the teams are in the B division.
