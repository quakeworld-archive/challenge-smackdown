Challenge Smackdown Australia

Group 1
Lies VS Mullets United


Round 1 - Wart

Mullets United 85
Lies 242
DM6 - The Dark Zone (Mullets United's choice)


Demos

POV: akbal

Report
For the second time this season MU only had 3 people available for a match, which means that they'll have to be careful which days they organise future games for as they'll need 4 players present from now on (this is a 4 on 4 league after all). Lies was nice enough to only field 3 guys in order to keep things even. When play began it was obvious that both teams would try to handle RA in the typical fashion...but on the other side of the map there were some differing tactics. MU would try to have a guy sitting in the mega room protecting the RL while a Lies player would look after it from GA. The bulk of the killing was done at RA though and this is where Lies built up their lead even though MU were able to take over there for short periods of time.



Round 2 - Wart

Lies 202
Mullets United 10
DM2 - Claustrophobolis (Lies's choice)


Demos

POV: akbal

Report
There was some confusion about whether dm2 was supposed to be played with deathmatch 3 or deathmatch 1 settings...though the brief discussion was held as soon as the game had started. With the focus back on the match, Lies made sure that RL's were a rare treat for MU as they held lower RL and the quad area nicely. MU seemed to be able to get players through YA and up to the quad RL without too many problems but they just weren't able to get out alive too often, and when they did they weren't in good condition for fighting. Near the end Akbal managed to go on a quad spree and this period provided roughly half of MU's final score which gives an indication of how clinical Lies were during the game.
