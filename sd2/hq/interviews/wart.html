Wart: Australian Division Admin - dK

You have adminned the first and second seasons of Smackdown for Australia, how has it been?

It's certainly been a learning experience. When matches are being played regularly it's great to follow how the teams progress...but when things aren't going as smoothly it can start to get a little frustrating. Overall I've enjoyed being a part of it.

You had your hands full with the Australian divison i'm sure but did you keep up with the North American and European divisions?

I tried to grab every demo from the other divisions as they appeared on CHTV and as the finals got closer I checked out each page to see if things were going well.

After keeping up with all divisions what two teams would you consider a great matchup?

As an Australian, I would love to see how an FA vs FF match would turn out...as well as FA vs AG. It's interesting how the NEU, NA and AU divisions have each had teams seemingly a step above their competitors.

Will you be adminning Smackdown 3 for Australia again this season?

Yep...I plan to improve on how the finals in AU have turned out...especially after the group games showed that we can get games played quickly if there's a system in place.

Would you like to take the time to thank everyone who has helped you with Smackdown?

Definitely. Porto, Sparky and Uther have done a great job with admining matches during SD2 and I hope to have them on the team again next season. They've also been helpful in discussing various issues as they pop up. Also, the division admins were another source of assistance as we all seem to go through similar problems at some stage.
