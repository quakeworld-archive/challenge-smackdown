DG vs FFF - zzjohnzz - 18/02/01

The match took a while because of certain players problems with qizmo. One player in particular finally got qizmo set up and upon doing an f_modified the screen filled with literally 50 mofified objects. I felt the guy would have to re-format to get rid of them all, but after a while everyone was ready to play.

DM6 would be played at drow where DG pinged 73 and FFF pinged 111, DM3 would be played at XX where DG pinged 70 and FFF pinged 93. The final map dm2 would be played at XX if necessary. DG chose dm6 trying to redeem themselves after being beaten there by GQ in their last match. The game was close for the 1st minute being a tie 7-7. Then after 2 minutes DG and their "ffa style" of play on dm6 opened up a 27-12 lead and never looked back. FFF seemed to try to camp the GA for a while but that didn't work. DG won 396-138. Snakes had 121 frags and daw finsihed with 113.

The unbelievable start of the year has to go to DG, they took everything on dm3: the rl, q/p/e, ra/ya and water weapons. FFF was left wondering what the heck just happened. DG was up 18-1 after 2 minutes and the map was locked tight. The only mistake was DG letting FFF get the pent at the 5 minute mark. The pent guy killed daw at quad, but really couldn't help his team get control. AT the 10 minute mark DG lead 90-14. The game ended 192-38 with daw running quad after quad showing some very nice timing. Cojer finished with 60 for DG and daw had 54.
