Another short update - Wart - 19/02/01

iC have come alive with two matches in two nights. They played THDM on Sunday night and just now (Monday night) a game with Dx was organised on short notice. Match reports for both of these games will be up soon but for anyone wanting to know the results now...iC won both without dropping a map.

The iC vs List and Azul vs Aok games are still outstanding but both groups of teams have assured me that they will be played asap (as long as teams keep admins informed of what's going on we'll do what we can to make sure matches go ahead). As for the next set of scheduled matches, they are as follows -

Group 1

  • Foad vs MU - Feb 21
  • Azul vs Rb - Feb 22
  • AoK vs Lies - Feb 23

Group 2
  • THDM vs FA - Feb 22
  • PoC vs iC - Feb 23

Dx have played all of their matches for the moment...if the replacement team for Justice is able to get organised very soon then they'll be scheduled to play them. We won't be waiting too long though as PoC and a few other teams are almost finished as well.
