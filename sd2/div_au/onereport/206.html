Group 2
Fallen Angels VS list of the twisted


Round 1 - Porto

Fallen Angels 199
list of the twisted 45
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Fallen Angels's choice)


Demos

POV: spice

Report
only the second match in the division not involving either dx or poc, last nite was fa vs. list.

dm3 saw fa get off to a good start and they attempted to hold down areas early into the game. before long spice was making quad runs and cleaning up respawned guys effectively without inflicting too much self damage. once the water area seemed in control vile made sure list didnt get a good look at the rl, and that respawned enemies didnt take the rl and run off the get armors and megah. list did play well however and often tried to get rid of spice, usually with an lg, and went close a number of times. when spice was unable to get quad another member made sure he got there in his place.



Round 2 - Porto

list of the twisted 111
Fallen Angels 155
E1M2 - Castle of the Damned (list of the twisted's choice)


Demos

POV: spice

Report
list chose e1m2 as fa made a lineup change, bringing in krim for vile. list did the same and replaced abc with noc. conoboy and beasil both had great starts, getting out to double figures quite early to establish a small lead for fa. spice managed to get an rl and time some quads soon after but his run was ended by sc camping at the megah. this got another list member an rl pack and between the two of them put together a number of frags in that area. the rl area was tightly contested with whatever weapon the opponents had, and basically the rl was up for grabs for the last man standing some of the time. ya didnt seem to be heavily camped, allowing people to come in and take an armor when they needed. the main thing in fa's favour during the match was the ability to get the majority of quads, with not just one person responsible for taking every quad. the lead was about 30 through the middle of the game and didnt change all that much in the second half.
