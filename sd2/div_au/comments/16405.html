Monday madness - Wart - 04/02/01

Sorry for the lame heading...but it looks like we might get through a number of games tomorrow night. At 8:00pm we've got FA vs PoC and Foad vs Lies set to happen followed by List vs Dx at 9:00pm. Assuming that every team involved is ready to go at their respective times we should be able to get through everything without causing major delays for the 9:00pm game. I would imagine that FA vs PoC could take place at IPGN while FOAD vs Lies goes ahead at Optus which would leave the List vs Dx game to start on whichever server is free first.

Admins shouldn't be a problem for each match...though maybe we'll see the IPGN Qizmo get a decent workout as people would be able to swap between each game without having to reconnect to different servers. All you would have to do is connect to the IPGN Qizmo (210.50.4.11:27505) and once in there you have two methods to observe the matches. If your keys are bound to use the menu then you can simply go to 'client list' and then move right to the 'observe' menu. Here you can select one of the admins watching the game (either Sparky, Porto, Uther or myself) and you'll then watch whoever we watch during the games. If your cursor keys don't have any effect on the menu, you can type .clients in the console which will bring up a list of people connected to the IPGN Qizmo. Find the one of the admins listed above and note the number next to their name. Type .observe "number" and you'll be watching the action. If anyone wants help with using public Qizmos to spectate matches feel free to add me to your icq list (3598769) and I'll give you a quick run through the process.

Most of the teams playing tomorrow night have had experience with Smackdown before so I'd be surprised if any Qizmo/f_modified related delays occur. Looking forward to some good games.
