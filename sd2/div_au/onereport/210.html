Challenge Smackdown Australia

Group 1
Fuck off and Die VS AoK


Round 1 - Wart

AoK 160
Fuck off and Die 192
DM6 - The Dark Zone (AoK's choice)


Demos

POV: hashie

POV: interceptor

Report
Needing to win this match to have any chance of advancing to the finals, AoK put in a good effort on dm6 against one of the Smackdown.AU favourites in Foad. They were ahead for the first 3 or 4 minutes but then Foad spread out over the map and racked up the spawns at GA, GL and RA. Both teams kept the scoreboard ticking over but Foad were adding to their frags a bit faster than AoK until about 7 minutes to go. Foad had double AoK's frags with the scores being around 140-70 but gradually AoK got hold of RA and soon they were the ones fragging faster. If they had started this run a bit sooner the result might have been different but Foad had enough on the board.



Round 2 - Wart

Fuck off and Die 118
AoK 25
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Fuck off and Die's choice)


Demos

POV: hashie

POV: darkone

POV: interceptor

Report
The nice attempted comeback on dm6 was the last time AoK were on top in this match as Foad only took a minute or so to get into their routine. Hashie looked after YA, Reign ran quad and Darkone (Alley) covered RA which basically left AoK with just SNG to try tank up at. Any loss of an area was only temporary as Reign would usually go to there on his next quad trip and it would soon be ready for his teammate to move in again. The most work Darkone seemed to have to do was guard the odd pack for Reign or Hashie when they died. This effectively ended AoK's Smackdown.AU season as they would have to rely on Rb getting beaten (by fair margins) at least twice in their last three games to make the finals...no such worries for Foad though.
