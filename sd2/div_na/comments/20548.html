It had to end sooner or later - zzjohnzz - 19/05/01

AG has never been defeated on any map in smackdown 1 or 2. Sure they won a game then had it taken away becuase KM-SKITTER forgot to use qizmo. But outside of KM-SKITTER'S mistake, AG has never lost a map. Def had to have dental surgery, fuman was at a wedding, haze and gibz play sometimes and don't play other times, Bull is missing in action so that just left me and yogic to try to take on GQ. With about 5 minutes until forfeit time, Punisher showed up and AG played GQ. On e1m2 GQ just controlled the board and pretty much crushed AG. Final score was 116-89. On dm3 Punisher got control of most of the quads and led AG to a 100-84 victory. Punisher had to leave and a drugged out def showed up. Back from the dentist and completely wasted out of his mind, def led AG to a 199-65 thrashing of GQ on e3m7. Then on dm2 AG couldn't get control of the lower rl all game. GQ would have 3 people at ra/mh and kept the game very close 104-84. AG wins 3-1 over GQ. Interestingly enough it was against GQ that KM-SKITTER cost AG their only loss in SD1.

One more thing, we played on the XTC server and everyone was plagued with 5-15 pl all game. Some of the GQ guys had even worse pl. I have no idea what is going on with that server but I hope they fix it soon enough.
