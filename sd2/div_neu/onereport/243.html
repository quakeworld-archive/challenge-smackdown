Challenge Smackdown North Europe

1/8 Finals
Euthanasia VS Clan Lithium


Round 1 - Apollyon

Euthanasia 203
Clan Lithium 81
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Euthanasia's choice)


Demos

POV: goljat

Report
The game took place on one of the eDome servers which gave quite fair pings to both teams with some slight advantages for Lithium. Before the match actually started there was some discussion going on. Unfortunately it was all swedish so i don't know what was the problem.
Euthanasia got the first rocketlauncher while Lithium got the quad. The quad was killed by some E boomsticker soon enough though. I am not sure who got the first pentagram (i think Lithium) but whoever took it he took it very late. Thus there was always some confusion before the next penta because they respawned much later than everyone was used to. After the first minute Lithium were leading 8-6 but that was the last time during this match were they were ahead of E. Goljat from Euthanasia took the second quad and secured RA. Soon they take over the lead but Lithium are around the the ya and rl area and even manage to take the 3rd quad. Despite that E secure ya though and increase their lead steadily. Vana made a lot of frags with the last quad before the next pentagram but still Lithium flooded the penta area and Baby Roo was able to take it. He did not have a rocketlauncher though. Meanwhile the score was 56 to 19 for Euthanasia. L's tried to secure RA but after some heavy fighting over it failed. In the following minutes E never manage to really lock down the map but still increase their lead while L makes only few frags. Then Lithium take the next pentagram again and after that even a few quads. Euthanasia are still on top of them though but L were gaining some frags now. After thirteen minutes they were still down with 93 frags. L are pushing E very hard now and but somehow Euthanasia always has that extra rocketlauncher ore more armor so that Lithium are never able to establish some sort of control over an area. Euthanasia take the last pentagram and the match is over. Two minutes before the end the score is at 176-73 and after taking the last quaddamage E win with a rather comfortable fragdifference
