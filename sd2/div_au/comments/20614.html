The iC situation - Wart - 22/05/01

On Saturday I was able to meet Teflon at Smegged and he assured me that iC would be playing their remaining three matches. Their opponents are FA, Rb and Azul...in theory we could get those games done pretty quickly as FA and Rb seem to be available on most nights while Azul just needs a few days notice. At the moment FA and Rb are seen as the likely Grand Finalists as iC would have to win all their games to take Rb's spot, though if they beat 2 out of the 3 teams they might still sneak in. Either way they would have to beat Rb and/or FA so, depending on the results, we'll end up with the top two teams in the Grand Final.
