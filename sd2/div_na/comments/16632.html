CAG vs FFF and ST vs RB - zzjohnzz - 11/02/01

At 1am only one FFF was on-line. CAG was asked if they wanted to reschedule and they said no. Cag's players have very busy schedules and some made sacrifices to be there that night. We waited 49 minutes and CAG won 2-0 by default.

The ST vs RB match took a while to get set up. After 40 minutes of qizmo troubles and random llamas jumping on the server and refusing to leave, the games got under way. RB were down a man, but did their best to play ST 3v4. RB chose dm3 and ST chose dm6. The random map would be dm2 if necessary. The first dm3 was a close and exciting game. ST won 80-60 powered by some excellent play by Titan. Unfortunately no one from ST recorded and RB asked that the game be played over.

The 2 teams were both HPB with average pings of 180 for ST and 220 for RB. The second dm3 was a blow out. ST got all the powerups and weapons and won 141-41. Former LOMO team leader Bubbz finished with an impressive 96.8% efficiency. Titan ran quad and finished with 56 frags and Exodus had 42. Crackerjack led RB both games with top frags for RB.

The dm6 game was intense at the start. Crackerjack had control of the RA and RB had a 53-40 lead after 5 minutes. Carnage must have been watching some Anti demos because after the 5 minute mark he decided to make the MH room his home. Defending the MH like a rabid pit bull in heat defends its territory, he slaughtered anything and everything he could dig his teeth into. This helped ST control the board at the 10 minute mark and they cruised to a 183-115 win. Carnage finished with 69 frags to lead all scorers. Crackerjack finished with an impressive 60 frags and had his team had 4 people, the games would have been more exciting.

Check out the hpb demos on challenge-tv. Tomorrow CG takes on CP3 at 10 pm EST. CG pings an average of 25 to may servers and CP3 has a few hpbs so chosing a server to play should be interesting to say the least. Check #smackdown on ETG for QTV details.
