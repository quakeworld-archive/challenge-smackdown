The Point Situation - JudgeDredd - 09/07/01

Things are a little too much bureaucratic down here, but group-games are not far away from it's end, and this should warm up everybody's vitalities. As all team leaders asked for me to post a complete report about all happenings, good or not, in this division, here is.

In Group 1 the Rage Clan, great championship contender, ceased their QuakeWorld activities for now, 8th Command of Brazil replaced them but already showed problems to handle with qizmo. Quakers in Black have an ok status, Clanalhas are ok also despite of give in a W.O. Hell Raisers' leader never more answered my calls and no other team member tried to search for me, I replaced them by the Black Hawks who will debut next weekend if everything runs well.

Group 2 has the RS Terminators with a regular situation, ClanBrazil ended with QuakeWorld for uncertain time, nobody will replace them. Papa-Figo is playing their games but there is a chance of them to be punished with unsportsman like conduct and Blood Revenge has the same situation of Clanalhas.

Group 3 all teams are ok with exception of Grave Diggers who are extinguished.

For games I updated all game reports with not having only it was a W.O. but with also the explanation of the situation that happened.

Until now I have witnessed W.O. after W.O. and this is more than unpleasant for me, a quake lover. Group 3 is finished, Animaniacs and Lords of War will advance for the playoffs. Group 2 has just one game left and looks like RS Terminators and Papa-Figo will advance too. Group 1 has work to do yet. It's easy to say Quakers in Black, RS Terminators and Animaniacs are the mainly championship contenders, the question is who will not get direct to the semis and have to play an extra-round, by the way, the reign man will be playing for the Terminators, Carlos. Another important question is that our division will no more be playing best of 5 rounds in the playoffs as it was anticipated but yes it will be single-round elimination, so it means we can have surprises in the way.
