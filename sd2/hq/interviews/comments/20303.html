NEU interviews part 2 - Apollyon - 11/05/01

All teams that made it into the playoffs of the north european division have been interviewed. Originally i wanted to ask 10 questions each but obviously i cannot count :-p .This articles includes all teams from the lower playoff bracket. The questions are as follows:

1. Are you pleased with your team's performance in the group games and your position?
2. What about your 1/8 final game? Do you think you can win it?
3. How far can you get if you survive the first round?
4. What was your favourite game so far in SD2?
5. Who will win SD2 ?
6. How did you like SD2 so far?
7. Was there anything you did not like about SD2 at all?
8. What about e3m7/dm6 in playoff games?
9. GL or VGA ?

Tero of Clan MalFunction:

1. Well, many people thought that we would be an easy team to beat this season, our flawless record proved them wrong so I´m satisfied.
2. Orkney is a tough team to beat, they have this superstar 'gore' who is really dangerous... but I suppose we can beat them.
3. We are, of course, aiming to win SD2, and we will :)
4. I haven´t really paid attention to other games but what I´ve heard TOT-AMD was pretty tight.
5. TVS is looking strong, they could take the title in front of FF,E and CMF! They surely are the favourites as they play the most.
6. SD2 has gone relatively smooth (comparing to last season) with most games being played etc.
7. It would be nice to have matchreports available from every game....some admins don´t upload them!
8. Well, we haven´t played those maps much (at all) in 4on4 for like two years.. so it would be better not to have them now when the games are more "important"
9. vga
