DM3: The Abandoned Base

From the Quad to the Pentagram and back to the centre through the water
by Jjonez

From the Quad its only a few strides to the window overlooking the outside area. A long drop down lands next to the Pentagram. Ignoring the elevators behind the powerup and the Megahealth in the distance, a cool plunge awaits. The long inundated tunnel leads to the Lightning gun. after a few more strokes the Grenade launcher is in sight and its high time to start breathing again. Getting on the low bridge in the Rocket launcher area, another brief walk ends in the central area. The tour is almost perfect, the Megahealth sits close below the Quad where it all started...

On to the third tour.
