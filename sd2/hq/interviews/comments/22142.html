Harlsom interview - Apollyon - 13/07/01

First of all congratulations for winning the CPL 4 Year anniversary event.
Thanx... i guess thats not a question is it :)

Please tell us a bit about yourself.
Harley Gray.I live in melbourne (which is in the south east of australia) and i'm a student at the moment. My biggest source of income at the moment is cpl so you could call me a pro gamer ;) But i was filling out a form i'd say student. I'm doing an arts course which is pretty slack so i've got more than enough time to keep up with any commitments as well as stay in shape in qw. Thats about it :)

How did you get into gaming and ultimately into QW?
Gaming itself... well when i was about 5 i went to my dads gym a lot just to bum around i guess (there isnt much to do when you're that age:]) and he had a microbee computer with a couple of games on it, i guess that was my first gaming experience :P
My first *real* game would probably be wing commander which i played at one of my friends places when i was in primary school so after that i nagged my parents about getting our own pc. As for getting into qw one of my friends lent me the cd one day in highschool, i didnt ask for it or anything and i hadnt even heard about 'quake' at the time so it was purely luck that i got it. Also i didnt have a modem or any way of playing multiplayer for the first year or so that i had it, so you could call me a pretty dedicated quaker :)
Well as soon as i got a modem i used one of those "free 20 hours internet connection" cd's and the first thing i searched for was not pr0n, but quake :)
So i found qw and used the whole 20 hours on it... after that ran out i proceeded to once again nag my parents about getting a net account, so yeah, thats how i got started :P

How did you feel like knowing that with the CPL 4 Year Anniversary you had just won the most important QW duel tournament since TGi?
At first i was just relieved that i pulled it off, and i wondered a bit about what it actually meant to win the event. Also i felt really good about winning because of all the support i got from the aussies. Seeing as reload didn't go, it was pretty much my responsibility to see that the au rep didnt get damaged.. :)
I never really thought about the cpl compared to tgi tho, it felt like i'd won an 'important event', but it felt like it was only important because it was the first time QW was recognised by more than the QW community. By the hardcore qw community standards, i have to admit the turnout was pretty poor, so in terms of that, the cpl was much less prestigious than tgi but yeah i was real happy nonetheless :)

Considering that many of the best QW duellers were not present at the event, how much does the victory mean to you personally?
Even tho none of the big name euros were there, it was still satisfying because i did what i went there to do. It was still difficult in a way, and being able to overcome some decent opposition when i wasnt aiming/moving so well was a good experience for me because my whole approach to each duel revolves around me being able to out-aim the opposition also it was just good to put the americans in their place :)

What do you think about those people saying that "every euro would own you silly on any map"?
Tell them to sponsor those euros to come to australia or sponsor me to go over there and we shall see :)

How much did you actually earn by winning the tournament and after substracting travel costs? What are you going to do with your winnings, some people said you should share parts of it with those who attended the fundraiser lan you won?
I havn't received the prizemoney yet so i'm not sure... at the moment though, i think USD $5k would exchange to AUD $9000-9500, travel costs were about AUD $2k so that leaves me with a nice little earner :))
I'm definately going to get a 19" monitor (at least;])
Also about sharing the $$$, when i won the smegged2 qualifier i was given 2 options, i could take $400 (runner up taking $200 i think) and leave it at that, or i could say "ok i'm going to cpl" and i could take all of the money raised for the lan.... So Blind came 2nd so he deserves some cash, shouldnt be too hard getting him to accept it. And also i need to find something to give to spice for wasting his whole weekend with me to practice qw :)
Also there is a lan coming up somewhere in europe it is called skaftlan and i'm really interested in going.They are having team comps there as well so maybe some of the cpl prizemoney could be put in to getting my clan there but thats just some crazy ideas being thrown around atm.

Where do you want qw to go? Do you want it to become popular as a progaming platform or do you prefer as it is now with people playing it just for fun?
I think it would be awesome if it became popular as any kind of platform, pro-gaming, played for fun, whatever. It was best like 2 years ago when the player base was still huge. Like, the smegged99 comp it was about as competitive as pro-gaming will ever get and that was the most fun lan i've ever been to. For qw, i dont think it will lose its fun no matter how 'pro' it becomes, there will always be people you can play with to have just fun games so... i want it to go wherever the most players lie :)

You wrote in your column that when you talked to Griff in Dallas he said that bunnyhopping ruined teamplay. Do you think he is right and how much does it affect duel?
Yes, i think he is right, many tactics are completely removed when people have the capability to speed jump...
As for duel i think that dm4 would be affected the least. Dm6 i also think it wouldnt matter so much because the most speed in dm6 is attained from starting with rocket jumps, not purely speed jumping. On dm2 there are a few more speed-jump reliant moves than dm6 so it would be annoying for that level so overall, duel levels are just too small for speed jumping to matter that much. What i was thinking it would be nice to try a mod where speed jumping acted like it does in NQ. Where you get speed from the first jump but not from the rest so if you RJ then you can keep the speed. It would just be annoying if servers reverted back to the pre-kteams type of standard duel mods. Those mods really annoyed me because as i said to Griff, as soon as i saw Sectopod bunnyhopping at TGi, i started practicing with a couple of players (Reac and Tr1gger). When i went to a duel comp at a lan, they werent running kteams servers, so i kept falling off platforms all the time, was really annoying...
So yeah, i suggest the nq type mod (not that it exists).

Are you of the conservative type preferring QW-Qizmo or do you like new clients like MQWCL?
Well, i'm conservative in that when i choose what i prefer, and then i stick to it. The first proxy i used was nf which i really liked, i used that for over a year and then smackdown started and i was forced to use qizmo which felt totally different, add to that the fact that i didnt like it. Yeah it sucked.
So taking that into account, i didnt really care about the new clients that much it was good that they were making some nice improvements tho. Then i noticed how they had FB skins in gl (i'm one of the few ppl who still play in gl) so i tried it out. I found that zquake worked real well with my nfproxy setup so i used that at smegged2. Unfortunately zq has stopped development apparently :(
I didnt like the mqw feel the first time i tried it, but seeing as sd2 is almost over and i wont be forced to use qizmo for a while, i started trying out mqw again, feels pretty nice now :)

Where do you see QW in a year from now on?
I think unless it somehow becomes hugely popular again, then more and more players will dissapear. I dont really know what its like over in europe but the NA scene is really tiny, the AU scene is much bigger, but still not that big. Also there arent really enough big name players left. Again, go back to smegged99 and the top 32 players there were all really skilled. Look at smegged2001 and compare the top32. Actually im not sure if there were even 32 people in the tourney :(
If qw continues the way its going - no big mainstream interest - then slowly the numbers will just fall away- There is some hope tho.I noticed at cpl the bigname q3 players -a great example is zero4, who had only been playing qw a couple of months- had some really nice aim. Fatality just didnt have enough experience to do as well as he does in q3 but if every quake(x) player changed that X to W, that would put enough fuel in the engine to give qw a couple more very interesting years at least :)

When does the AU final take place, are you going to play and who will win it of course?
*Hopefully* tomorrow night, probably around the same time as we're doing this interview (15:00 CET). Maybe an hour or two later. Yes i'm going to play, and of course FA is going to win it :)

How did you like SD2 and what do you expect from SD3?
The au div. of sd2 went along much smoother than the first, the only bumps my clan ran into was the two times we had to play clan icey. The first time they kept calling it off and not showing up etc, we figured that it was because they didnt want to play without Reload or Dude (who was on DSL at the start of the season getting 40-50 ping), eventually when we DID play, Reload wasnt on the team and dude was on dialup, their bad luck. We even copped a bit of flak(=abuse) for forcing them to play without their best squad. Heh but, enough about them.
The season was all round really well run. Instead of taking everyone 2hrs to fix their qizmo it was more like 10mins
I'm not really sure about sd3, depends what the client/proxy situation is going to be, coz thats where all the trouble arises. It seems like a really simple thing to set it up but some people just dont/cant/wont do it because they dunno how/cbf/forgot to/think they can just ignore the rules, really fkn annoying. But, seeing that it was much less a problem in sd2 than sd1 maybe it will become insignifigant in sd23,err sd3 :)
Or maybe diff clients/etc will be allowed i have no idea. Also i'm wondering about whether smackdown will remain a TeamDM only league, or if it will expand or fully switch to other formats eg ctf/2on2 i dunno. There have been a couple of duel/2on2 comps *organised* in au over the past year. I think the besmella duel comp started a whole year ago, 16 players, still not finished. I'll have to check that out, it could go in guiness book of records :)

Did you follow the other divisions and what do you think about them?
Well...Blind summed up the NA division really well after the sd1 season. He said something like "i watched one of the NA demos for 2mins, they couldnt even do the jump across eyes/quad on dm3." I watched most of the eu demos tho.
Hehe i played with them a couple of times and believe me they can do it nowadays :)
From clans like FF, E, as well as some TVS, also Rat from D. Yeah i noticed at cpl they knew how to hop. I actually did watch the finals games of the NA div if i remember correctly it was 3on3. I hate 3on3s. Add to that, each game was a HUGE blowout. Shame that they dont have enough players to make more than one decent team. It'd be interesting if cK played in SD3. Fatality and czm IMO would be enough to take down AG if they have another two decent players on the squad.
With the euros it got kinda boring to watch this season. I remember the first time i watched Pietro play, aussies had never seen teamplay like that before. Also the finals of E vs FF were awesome to watch but for some reason even the close games between good clans this season, were kinda boring. Its like we've seen it all now but its still a heap of fun to play. That i think, will never change :)

If you could choose freely, which player would you like to play against (and beat him eventually)?
Yee, well there are a couple of answers to that question :)
Everyone will tell you that after they play against Reload, they play a whole lot better, so for the purpose of improving i'd choose him. But i've already played him a few times. So i'd choose either Timber or Kane. I really couldn't decide who. Griff tells me they pretty much completely stopped playing QW a little while after rapture99 due to q3 sponsorship etc but if i could play them when they were at the level of skill they were back then, that would be the best.
Oh and also FA would really love to play FF on lan someday :)

Thanks alot for taking the time
No probs... was fun
