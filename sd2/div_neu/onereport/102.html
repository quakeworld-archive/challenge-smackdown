Clan MalFunction VS Armageddon Deep Town 2


Round 1 - Andy

Clan MalFunction 290
Armageddon Deep Town 2 28
DM3 - The Abandoned Base (Clan MalFunction's choice)


Demos

POV: dare

POV: peksi

Report
That game started about 30 minutes later than scheduled because some ADT players had problems getting the bus or something. CMF agreed to wait though, so the game took place a bit later. It was agreed to play that match on a get2net server. Not surprisingly, CMF from Finland had a little ping advantage of about 10ms, not as much as i had expected though. ADT's line showed some problems today, giving pl spikes to 15 from time to time but all in all it seemed playable from ADT's POV. dm3 would be CMF's map, e1m2's ADT's and dm2 the tiebreaker if needed. There's not too much to report about the dm3 game, really :( I think ADT got away with the rl only at the start, which was killed by CMF's quadman on the way to YA. It didn't take too long to get control over the map for CMF. OK, ADT sometimes attacked RA and a few times even succeeded in taking over but that changed at latest when CMF's quad guy came in and cleaned it again. Sometimes CMF had some luck with the Pents (or just 100% exact timing) but CMF's victory was never in any danger. CMF beat ADT2 290-28 on dm3



Round 2 - Andy

Clan MalFunction 218
Armageddon Deep Town 2 120
E1M2 - Castle of the Damned (Clan MalFunction's choice)


Demos

POV: dare

Report
There are times when screenshots don't tell the truth (unfortunately there's no screenshot because i don't understand how that new script works so "sometimes scores don't tell the truth" :p). CMF got an 3l33t start, taking almost all positions very quickly. They were able to gain a huge frag advantage, which seemed to unpossible to beat. And that after just 7 minutes. But ADT hit back hard and seemed to like the place at the YA. They controlled it for almost the total rest of the game and occassionally ran the quad too. However, it wasn't too easy for ADT to use their advantage of controlling YA that successful but it was enough to gain a lot of frags and make the final result look better. CMF's victory never seemed to be in real danger on e1m2 either but i wished we had seen an equal start, and i'm sure we'd have seen a very close thriller. Ded timed about 30 seconds before the end with 30 frags. The final score was 218-120. nice game
