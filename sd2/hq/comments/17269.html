More QW stuff - Apollyon - 01/03/01

Hm, ist it just me or is QW really getting stronger lately?
Well i have some interesting links for you today. First there is another good article by Hoony on challenge-world called "When Ninja's meet" where he outlines what makes players stars.
A few days ago an new QW news site emerged which is called Prewar. They are going to post QW news and articles only and they want to become the first adress for anything qw related. An interesting site to follow.
I discovered yet another QW news page. This one is called Quakezone and comes from some new zealanders i think. This page has a veery nice and complete qw file section. They even offer Quake for download! I wonder for how long though since this is illegal ;-)
Finally Janus pointed me to a site which is about the old days of Quake in norway. So if you like to know how people played quake in norway back in 96/97 go here and have a look.
