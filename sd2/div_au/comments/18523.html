Almost there - Wart - 20/03/01

We've only got one more match to go before we go into finals mode. Rb vs MU is the game we're waiting on as Lies have informed me that they forfeit their match against Rb due to a lack of players. MU are just trying to get 4 guys online in order to play Rb...I'm willing to give them until Saturday as I'm sure the finalists are eager to get into the next lot of matches asap.

On Sunday night the last game in group 2 between iC and FA went ahead...eventually. A number of dedicated spectators waited on the ipgn qizmo in the hope that they would see a game and in the end it was played with FA winning on dm6 and dm3. The match report will be posted shortly. Speaking of match reports...the old ones have been transferred into the new backoffice. You can check them out by going to the 'Reports' link on the left. The rankings are generated from the data in the match reports so the last few matches have to be added to get an accurate reading.

We'll post the first round of the finals on the weekend (whether the MU vs Rb game gets played or not) and I'll give the predictions another shot.
