Small preview - Apollyon - 15/06/01

Tomorrow we will see the second but last match of this season in northern europe. It will be between Euthanaia and Clan MalFunction. Both teams have been around for ages, have been quite sucessfull in their careers and are among the best teams in europe for sure. There are more stories to tell: Euthanasia were last seasons runner ups and are looking forward to reach the final of the most important european league for the third time in a row - and to win it this time. Clan MalFunction on the other hand are the only team that has not lost a single map during the whole tournament yet.
I think we are going to see one of the most exciting matches of the tournament tomorrow. Chances are good that it will be much more exciting than the somewhat ill fated semifinal between FF and TVS. I am sure that the interest will be just as high. That means full qizmo's all over northern europe.
I am not sure if CMF will be able to play from lan tomorrow but if so they will be a threat to Euthanasia for sure. They lanned last week and did pretty well except in the one deciding match versus TVS. I am sure they learned from it. On the other hand what can go wrong if you have Darklord, Goljat, Paradoks and Vana on the same team?
Ok i think i should finally get to the point of this update: Don't miss this game tomorrow! Thanks for your attention :-)
