Report
DM2 (OD's choice)
I thought I would escape the server trouble for this match, because both clans are finnish. But the finnish teamserver had PL this night. Imagine that. I can't recall the last time I've seen those finnish servers lag. Of course tonight they had to and we were off to a danish server first, then to a swedish one, to finally play in finland after all.
OD grabbed the lower RL and high RL, while NS got the quad. OD secured tele, but NS fights back and takes control. They get an early lead. OD isn't going down easy though and takes tele again. They start to eat up NS lead and get the lead when it's 13 minutes left to play with 48-44 in frags. The server give some annoying pl to all players and makes it difficult to get control of the whole map. NS fights back hard, but OD manages to sort it out and ends up with control. After taking more quads than their opponents, OD defeats NS on DM2.
