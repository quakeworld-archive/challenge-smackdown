Spanish QW Clan League - Gacel - 04/05/01

The Spanish QuakeWorld League (Site in spanish) is running another season. This time the league will include maps for playing 5 vs 5, like E4M5 - Hell's Atrium and E4M7 - Azure Agony among others (of course 4 vs 4 maps like DM2 and DM3 will be included too).

The deadline for registering is next 20 May.

If you are insterested in joining it then send a mail to leqwcomite@yahoogroups.com with the following information:

  • Clan Name
  • Clan Tag
  • Clan URL
  • Clan Mail
  • Clan Leader
  • List of members with email of everyone
Of course QIZMO is mandatory. Remember that your clan must have at least 5 active members.

If you are interested in clanwars played on non-standard maps and you don't ping too high to qw.area66.com then you should join the competition ;)
