It are time to frag! - Apollyon - 20/04/01

Ok, pick up your rocket launchers, shoulder your backpacks, heat up your shafts and most important of all: turn off yer gib filters! It is time to find out who is northern europes top fragger. And he will be decided this very night.

The groups have been decided by a random draw and look as follows:

Group 1
Group 2
Group 3
Group 4

The rules of the tournament are:
All players meet in #smackdown.
Each group of 17 players will play two times 20 minutes of death32c. The 4 guys who had the most frags combining the results of both maps advance to the final round which will take place on death32c as well, this time 30 minutes. The topfragger of the final round is the overall winner. If two players are tied on frags (like 4th and 5th in one group) they will both advance and the guy with the least frags from another group won't.
The server will be set to dmm3, teamplay 2 and powerups off.
Smackdown rules apply (so no other clients are allowed) but we will not do all the f_checks. This is not misunderstood to be an encouragement of breaking the SD rules! Any offenders will be kicked from the server and disqualified.
Spectators should connect through Qizmos so that more than 10 people can spec.
Players not showing up will be replaced by anyone willing to take part and being present in #smackdown.
All players are encouraged to record demos, spectators should try to catch some nice high resolution screenshots. Both will be used to show the world that QW is the best game once and for all :-)
