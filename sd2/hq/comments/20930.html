Challenge Smackdown Headquarters


Site overview - Jjonez - 01/06/01

With the finals drawing near in most divisions, I think it wouldn't hurt to give a brief overview of the information available on the Challenge Smackdown sites.

First of all there are the pages for each division. You can find the links on the left here, below the general information. There's South Europe and North America which have dates set for their respective finals, North Europe, which has started quarter finals, Australia with just a few games remaining before playoffs and Brazil which is, due to continued connection problems, only about to start.
Once you're on such division page, the panels on the left will change and you'll be able to check out information specific to the division like the list teams participating, an overview of the results and a list of the played games. Most divisions provide extensive reports for all games. Check ou some of those, we're rather proud of our reports (and of the admins writing them up!).
In the reports you'll also find links to demos you might want to download. The older reports have link to the central repository on Challenge-TV, while the latest ones (from mid-May onwards) have direct links to the download page.

Here on headquarters, you can find our Challenge Smackdown FAQ or if you're trying to follow the games online while they're happening a more extensive Qizmo speccing guide.
Before you attempt following a game, you might also be interested to take our guided tours around the league maps.

On every page you'll also find links to columns maintained by players and admins.

Happy QW'ing!
