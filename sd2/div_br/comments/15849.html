MatchDay #1 - JudgeDredd - 21/01/01

We are moments away from starting group-games, ClanBrazil vs Belo Horizonte Team will be the match. Unfortunately Hell Killerz is leaving Smackdown.Brazil and this means we have only one game today. ClanBrazil is coming back to activity since September last year, and Belo Horizonte Team (Belo Horizonte is the state capital name's team) are participating in their 2nd major event. Belo Horizonte is new on the house anyway and ClanBrazil it's impossible to predict how they will play, so this will be interesting.
