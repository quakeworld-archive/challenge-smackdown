Demos

Report
The dm2 game started and Gunandahalf crashed once again in the first minute of the game. At this point AoK had a small lead but after some discussion the game was restarted because he crashed so early in the game. The dm2 game got restarted as soon as gunandahalf got back. This dm2 game prooved to be one very exciting game and i recommend getting a demo of this game when they come out. AoK once again got to an early lead and managed to get around 30 frags ahead... Azul then came back, took the lead and then got a similar lead over AoK. Towards the end of the game the scores were very close with AoK having enough lead to win, but not without Azul lowering the margin from about 15 to 4 in the dying seconds. Porto top scoring for Azul this match even though they lost.
