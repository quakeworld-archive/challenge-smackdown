Playoffs soon, PLAY your games! - andy - 30/04/01

Just a reminder: Group games of N-EU will finish at 5th of May. That means, that games that weren't played until then won't count (unless you got a game scheduled for the next days, there will be a one week break after postseason for games that are scheduled). No game - no scores (lo Group C). Group C looks really bad, 11 more games to be played. Quite the opposite can be seen in Div A, all games apart from one have been played (D vs TFP is not playoff relevant anymore) and the clans of Group A which will proceed to the playoffs are already determined:

1.FF
2.FS
3.TDI
4.OC

Group B has had most games played, but playoffs spots are still to be won. Most of those relevant games will be played this week and i'm sure it will become really tight. So far, only TVS are qualified from Group B as the clear winner, one map game to be played, but this can't change their position at the top anymore. Koff and RC are also qualified for the playoffs, still unsure what place they will finish at though. It can't be said who also will proceed, it will be either DN or L.

Pfffffff group c! CMF are the only clan who are already qualfied. Toooooooooooooo many games still aren't played so it could all change dramatically. For example, DSG could still become 2nd, they only played 2 games so far and i wonder if they're (and some other Group C clans) are still alive. By the looks of it CMF, NS, OD and 4K will be the 4 that will make it into playoffs, simply because of other clans being lazy cunts.

1. Euthanasia

HF might not be as motivated as they used to be as they're already qualified as 3rd, but we will see.
