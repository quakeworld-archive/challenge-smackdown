Report
DM2 (tvs's choice)

This map was played on a finnish server.
Tvs spawns lower RL and spas gets high RL and quad. Spas secures tele and take the next quad, but the quadrunner doesn't have a weapon and is soon killed. Tvs launches an attack and takes the third quad. Spas leads however with 25-15 with 17 minutes remaining. Now it's spas turn to grab quad and they secure tele yet again. Spas never manages to secure the lower RL though and tvs is still in the game. Still spas has a narrow lead when it's 10 minutes left to play. When it's 8 minutes remaining, tvs takes over the map and starts spawnfraging. With 6 minutes left, tvs leads by 49 frags, but then spas steals quad and starts to eat up tvs lead. 152-135 is the score with 3 minutes remaining, but tvs battles it out and in the end wins by a 40 frag margin.

Report
E1M2 (spas's choice)

Now it was time to change the server and in the end we landed on a danish one. It took some time to get ready because two of the tvs guys suffered from PL (you got to love leechers).

Tvs gets a nice start on this one and gets both quad and RL. After 2 minutes they are 18 frags up and have control over the YA/GL area. Things seem to be going well for tvs, but 5 minutes in to the game two of their guys start to lag. Tvs struggles on though and leads 82-41 with 14 minutes remaining. Spas manages to reduce their lead to 33 frags with 11 minutes left to play. After that Acid drops out with 20 frags and is gone for a couple of minutes. Tvs sorts things out with the lag coming and going and with 4 minutes left to play they lead by 67 frags with no chance for spas to catch up.
