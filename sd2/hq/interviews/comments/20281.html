NEU interviews part 1 - Apollyon - 10/05/01

All teams that made it into the playoffs of the north european division have been interviewed. Originally i wanted to ask 10 questions each but obviously i cannot count :-p .This articles includes all teams from the upper playoff bracket. The questions are as follows:

1. Are you pleased with your team's performance in the group games and your position?
2. What about your 1/8 final game? Do you think you can win it?
3. How far can you get if you survive the first round?
4. What was your favourite game so far in SD2?
5. Who will win SD2 ?
6. How did you like SD2 so far?
7. Was there anything you did not like about SD2 at all?
8. What about e3m7/dm6 in playoff games?
9. GL or VGA ?
